Radar engineers discuss the iterative development of HorizonDB, their in-house geospatial database handling over a billion daily API requests. The post details a two-tier architecture combining FST (finite state transducers) for fast prefix matching with Tantivy inverted indices for deeper searches, plus optimization techniques for indexing 500 million labels.
