Location spoofing occurs when devices report false coordinates to circumvent location-based restrictions across rideshare, delivery, payments, and retail platforms. Traditional identity and transaction monitoring systems fail to detect this fraud; effective detection requires combining device integrity checks, GPS signal consistency verification, and movement analysis.
