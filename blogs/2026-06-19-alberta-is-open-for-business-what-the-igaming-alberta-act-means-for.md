Alberta's regulated online gambling market launches July 13, 2026, with nearly 50 licensed operators replacing the previous government-exclusive model. Operators must implement continuous compliance measures including account sharing detection, self-exclusion screening, and real-time location verification to ensure all wagering occurs within provincial borders.
